## 8. How to work this

**Where to check**
- freejobalert.com/bank-jobs/ and freejobalert.com/government-jobs/ — the two pages that have caught everything so far
- bankersadda.com — bank-specific, often a day earlier
- Official: ibps.in · sbi.bank.in/careers · rbi.org.in · ssc.gov.in · rrbapply.gov.in · hssc.gov.in · hpsc.gov.in · nationalinsurance.nic.co.in · careers.cdac.in
- Watch for clone domains: sarkariresult.com.cm, .app and .im are SEO copies carrying stale dates

**Rules learned the hard way**
- **Always verify the experience clause in the official PDF before paying.** Every miss came from an aggregator listing "Any Graduate" while the notification buried a 1–3 year requirement.
- **Lead with the nearest deadline**, not the most valuable post. A form was once dropped from a list because it was ranked by value instead.
- **Carry the full open list forward** each time rather than rebuilding it from the latest search — items fall off otherwise.
- Keep lists **short and ranked**. He asks about openings often but acts rarely, so naming the single best one works better than listing everything open.

**His stated preferences**
- Show only the **immediate next exam stage** — no mains or interview dates until the prelims is cleared.
- No physical tests.
- Salary figures with every suggestion.
